# Business Problem

## Project

AI-Powered Customer Support & Case Prioritization

## 1. Background

A global organization receives customer support cases through multiple channels, including email, web forms, and customer portals.

Support teams handle cases related to product issues, technical problems, account requests, billing questions, and service disruptions.

As the number of customer cases increases, manually reviewing and prioritizing every case becomes time-consuming and can lead to inconsistent prioritization.

## 2. Problem Statement

Customer support cases are not always consistently classified and prioritized based on severity, customer impact, and business urgency.

This can result in critical customer issues being treated with the same urgency as routine requests.

The business requires a structured approach to classify incoming cases, determine priority, route cases to the appropriate support team, and monitor SLA deadlines.

## 3. Business Impact

The current process may result in:

- Delayed response to critical customer issues
- SLA breaches
- Incorrect case assignment
- Increased manual effort for support agents
- Inconsistent case prioritization
- Increased customer dissatisfaction
- Limited visibility into support performance
- Difficulty identifying recurring support issues

## 4. Business Objective

The objective is to define requirements for an intelligent customer support case management solution that can assist with:

- Case classification
- Priority determination
- Customer impact assessment
- Support team routing
- SLA tracking
- Case escalation
- Support performance reporting

## 5. Expected Business Outcomes

The proposed solution should help the organization:

- Reduce manual case classification effort
- Improve prioritization of critical customer issues
- Route cases to appropriate support teams
- Reduce SLA breaches
- Improve response and resolution times
- Provide better visibility into support operations
- Improve the overall customer support experience

## 6. Key Business Questions

The following questions should be answered during requirement gathering:

1. What types of customer cases are received?
2. How is case priority currently determined?
3. What makes a case critical?
4. How is customer impact determined?
5. Which teams should handle different case categories?
6. What are the SLA requirements for each priority?
7. When should a case be escalated?
8. What information should be captured when a case is created?
9. Should AI recommendations be reviewed by a support agent?
10. What KPIs should management monitor?

## 7. Success Criteria

The solution will be considered successful if it enables the business to consistently classify and prioritize customer cases, route them to the appropriate teams, monitor SLA commitments, and identify cases requiring escalation.
