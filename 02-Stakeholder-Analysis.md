# Stakeholder Analysis

## Project

AI-Powered Customer Support & Case Prioritization

## Purpose

Identify the stakeholders involved in customer support case management and understand their responsibilities, needs, influence, and involvement in the proposed solution.

## Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Role | Key Responsibilities | Key Needs | Influence | Involvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer | External User | Reports issues and requests support | Fast and accurate resolution | Medium | High |
| Support Agent | End User | Reviews, manages, and resolves cases | Clear priority and correct routing | High | High |
| Support Manager | Business Stakeholder | Manages support operations and team performance | SLA visibility and workload management | High | High |
| Product Team | Product Stakeholder | Defines product behavior and priorities | Clear business requirements | High | High |
| Business Analyst | Requirements Owner | Gathers, analyzes, and documents requirements | Clear and validated requirements | High | High |
| Development Team | Technical Team | Designs and develops the solution | Clear functional requirements | High | High |
| QA Team | Testing Team | Validates solution against requirements | Testable requirements and acceptance criteria | Medium | High |
| AI / Data Team | Technical Stakeholder | Develops and maintains classification logic/model | Data requirements and business rules | High | Medium |
| IT / System Administrator | Technical Stakeholder | Maintains system integrations and access | Technical and security requirements | Medium | Medium |
| Business Management | Decision Maker | Reviews business outcomes and approves major changes | KPIs, cost, and operational impact | High | Medium |

## Stakeholder Needs

### Customers

Customers expect:

- Faster responses
- Correct handling of issues
- Appropriate priority for critical problems
- Clear communication
- Reduced resolution time

### Support Agents

Support agents need:

- Clear case categories
- Recommended priority
- Customer impact information
- Correct team assignment
- SLA deadlines
- Escalation notifications

### Support Managers

Support managers need:

- Visibility into open cases
- SLA performance
- Priority distribution
- Team workload
- Escalated cases
- Resolution trends

### Product Team

The Product Team needs:

- Clearly defined requirements
- Business rules
- Feature scope
- Acceptance criteria
- Customer impact information

### AI / Data Team

The AI / Data Team needs:

- Case classification categories
- Historical case data requirements
- Priority rules
- Data quality expectations
- Model output requirements
- Human review requirements

## Business Analyst Responsibilities

The Business Analyst acts as the bridge between business stakeholders and technical teams.

Key responsibilities include:

- Conduct stakeholder discussions
- Gather and analyze requirements
- Identify stakeholder needs and expectations
- Define business rules
- Identify process gaps
- Document functional requirements
- Clarify AI-related business expectations
- Define user stories and acceptance criteria
- Support UAT
- Facilitate communication between business and technical teams
- Ensure requirements remain aligned with business objectives
