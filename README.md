# AI-Powered Customer Support & Case Prioritization – Business Analyst Case Study

## Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates the Business Analysis lifecycle for an AI-powered customer support case management solution.

The solution is designed to help organizations classify, prioritize, route, and escalate customer support cases based on issue severity, customer impact, and defined Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

## Business Problem

Global customer support teams may receive a high volume of customer cases across different products, regions, and severity levels.

Without a structured prioritization and routing process:

- Critical cases may not receive immediate attention
- Cases may be assigned to the wrong support team
- SLA breaches may occur
- Support teams may spend additional time manually categorizing cases
- Management may have limited visibility into support performance

## Business Objective

Define business and functional requirements for a solution that can:

- Classify incoming customer cases
- Determine case priority
- Identify customer impact
- Route cases to the appropriate support team
- Track SLA deadlines
- Escalate cases approaching or exceeding SLA
- Provide visibility into support performance
